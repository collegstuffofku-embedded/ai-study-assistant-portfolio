1. Core Intuition

Ohm's Law states that the electric current (I) flowing through a metallic conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference (V) across its ends, provided temperature and physical conditions remain constant.

2. Fundamental Equations

  • Voltage–Current Relation: V = I × R (Volts, V)
  • Electrical Power Dissipated: P = V × I = I²R = V²/R (Watts, W)

3. Quick Solved Example

Problem: A 12 V DC source is connected across a 4 Ω resistor. Find the current and power dissipated.
Solution: I = 12 V / 4 Ω = 3 A, and P = 12 V × 3 A = 36 W.
